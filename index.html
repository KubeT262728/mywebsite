How to play Crazy Eights

Crazy Eight is a classic game of cards that's easy to learn, fast to learn ,and fun for all ages. Whether you're using Regal Games Crazy 8's Card Game or a standard 52-card deck, the standard rules stay mostly the same. Players take turns matching their cards in ranks or suits, using Eights as powerful wild cards to change the flow of the game.

This guide explains the object of this game, setup , gameplay rules, winning conditions , penalties, popular variations, so you can start playing right away.
